Palette

Choose a color, then choose a space on the board.

How to play

Use nine of the twelve colors to fill the center grid. Three colors are decoys.

The left circle is the target. As colors are added, the circle on the right blends their colors.

  • Each right-side circle blends the colors in its row.
  • Each bottom circle blends the colors in its column.
  • Match every blend to its target circle to complete the puzzle.
